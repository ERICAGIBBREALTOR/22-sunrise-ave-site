About This Home

A Rare Combination on This Stretch of Coast

Recent improvements, genuine character, and authentic proximity to the water.

The Essence

Thoughtfully Maintained, Ready to Move In

This solid block home delivers the specific improvements today's buyers seek and often cannot find ready-made: a new HVAC system installed in 2026, updated electrical panel and wiring with new service riser, new soffit and fascia completed in 2025, and new siding on the east exposure installed in 2026. The exterior has been professionally power-washed, and the seller is including extra matching roof tiles, siding materials, and touch-up paint for future maintenance.

Inside, 1,520 square feet of thoughtfully arranged space includes a bright living room and a separate bonus living area—genuine square footage for everyone to spread out. Beautiful terrazzo flooring runs throughout the bedrooms and primary living spaces, and the efficient galley kitchen combines authentic period character with honest functionality.

The primary suite features a spacious walk-in cedar closet and private en-suite bathroom. A generously proportioned hallway bathroom serves the guest bedrooms. The enclosed Florida room—freshly repainted and filled with light—is where mornings and quiet afternoons naturally happen. Locals call this stretch of coast "the island," and once you're here, you'll understand why.

The Outdoor Space

Room to Live Beyond the Walls

The fully fenced backyard is expansive enough for a pool or any creative outdoor vision you hold. An irrigation well on the property means lawn maintenance stays simple and worry-free. Whether it's your morning coffee in the Florida room or an evening under the sky, the setting remains quiet—just birdsong and the peaceful rhythms of island living.

  • Fully fenced backyard, spacious enough for a pool
  • Irrigation well installed for simplified lawn care
  • Enclosed, recently repainted Florida room
  • Just 8 homes from the public no-drive beach access
  • Built on solid block foundation (1966)
  • No homeowners association fees

The Beach

No-Drive Beach Access

Just eight homes separate 22 Sunrise Ave from the beach. This is a genuine no-drive area, meaning no commercial parking lots, no boardwalks lined with shops. You walk across sand on your own property and find the Atlantic Ocean exactly as it's been for centuries.

The sand here is natural and wide, the crowd is genuine beachgoers rather than tourist traffic, and the sound you hear most is the rhythm of waves. This isn't a destination for tourism—it's a home for living.

  • No-drive beach, 8 homes from your door
  • Quiet, natural sand beaches
  • Sea turtle nesting habitat in season
  • Walking and biking distance to everything
